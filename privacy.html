Clip Watcher - Privacy Policy Last updated: 18 November 2026

Clip Watcher is a private tool used only by the owner of the Streamer Highlights accounts to upload the owner's own short highlight clips to the owner's own TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.

What we access: when the owner connects their TikTok account, Clip Watcher receives an access token and basic account information (open ID, display name) from TikTok, used only to upload videos to that same account's drafts / inbox.

What we store: the access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and we do not access other users' accounts or data.

Third parties: no data is shared with anyone. Videos are sent only to the platform they are posted on.

Deleting data: the owner can disconnect Clip Watcher at any time in TikTok's settings (Security > Manage app permissions); the stored token is then deleted from the computer.
